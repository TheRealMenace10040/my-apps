# Surface: My Apps launcher (public/index.html)

Mode: Operate. Audience: Dennis, phone home screen first, desktop second. Job: open one of his apps within a second or two. Constraints: static, apps.js stays a one-object edit, PWA keeps working. Avoid: looking plain.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The launcher is a hotel front-desk key board: every app is a coloured key fob on a brass hook, numbered by row. It refuses the category default of a grey grid of same-size icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal board (#18191b) with a soft lamp falloff, no grain; brass for rules, per-slot hooks, index tabs and focus. Fobs are moulded plastic/metal in four group colours (Shared copper, Play slate, Tools pewter with dark ink, Work brass with dark ink), stamped numerals. Amended after the first build: Dennis said "i dont like the color scheme" of the walnut version and picked option "D · Charcoal and metal" from four alternatives; Tools moved from graphite to pewter so the rows stay distinguishable. Archivo at wide and condensed widths; one text size on fobs, rank by weight and case.

STORY: He sees the whole board, recognises the row by colour, taps the fob, and the app opens in a new tab.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone 390 wide: "My Apps" wordmark with brass sub-line, a sticky brass thumb index (Shared, Play, Tools, Work), then the Shared row of two-column fobs; the Play row starts above the fold. Desktop: centred board, rows four to five fobs wide.

FORM: Motel Key Board, assigned candidate 4 of 7. seed:452b7d99ae78e514. Signature interaction: a fob swings on its hook on hover and press; on desktop, typing a fob's two-digit hook number opens it.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
